## Annual Meeting 2026
The sixth meeting of London Genetics Network was held on 8th January 2026, exploring the theme “Vanishing boundaries between rare and common genetic variation.” The meeting featured scientific talks spanning rare and common variant genetics, from methodological advances to clinical and population-scale applications, highlighting the growing convergence between these traditionally distinct areas of genetic research.
